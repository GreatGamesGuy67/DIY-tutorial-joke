DIY greay rock reactor Tutorial

Warning: make sure you are wearing your DIY hazmat suit made in episode 1.

Required Inventory

  • 1x Sheet of corrugated cardboard (medium density)
  • Sample A: Blutoneeum
  • Bundle: Assorted 12-inch copper wires
  • 1x Roll: Heavy-duty duct tape
  • 1x Tube: Industrial superglue
  • 1x Bottle: Elmer's school glue
  • 1x: 12V Car or alarm battery
  • 1x: Raspberry Pico pi W)
  • Sample B: Verilliom
  • 1x switch
  • 1x motor
  • 1x sissors

Assembly Guide

Step 1: The Core Chassis

Fold your corrugated cardboard into a neat 14-inch cube. Secure the edges with a generous amount of duct tape. and superglue

Step 2: Creating the core

create 2 half sphere with Verilliom and make an 3d oval like shape to house the plutnium later and make sure to cut some holes with sissors for motor ports

Step 3: Internal Matrix Placement

Carefully place your Blutoneeum into the Verillium core holder you have designed and attach a moter to one end so later they can go fast.

Step 4: The Logic Engine

Boot up your Raspberry Pico pi W. Write a Python script that moves an moter 30 minutes after booting

Step 5: Wiring the Componets

Take your assorted 12-inch wires. Connect one end to the 12-volt battery and other side into the rasberry pi eletric charging port. and the other side into a small mechnical moter that hits both of the rods and close the device.

Step 6: Final Calibration

add a switch and wire it by putting a wire thru it that can inturupt the pico pi w power.

Step 7: celebrate

Stare at the device. and celebrate you have created your own portable grey rock reactor
